<!-- SECOND BAPTISM -->
<div class="confession-baptism-panel">
  <h2 class="confession-baptism-title">The "Second Baptism"</h2>
  <p>The Church Fathers call confession a <strong>"second baptism"</strong>, not because it repeats baptism (which can only happen once), but because it <strong>restores what baptism originally gave us</strong>. At baptism, we were washed clean and made children of God. Confession is how that garment is washed again.</p>

<!-- THE RITE -->
<h2 class="rite-steps-label">The Six Steps</h2>

<div class="rite-stepper">
  <div class="rite-progress"><div class="rite-progress-bar" style="width:16.7%"></div></div>

  <section class="rite-stage">
    <div class="rite-stage-head">
      <div class="rite-stage-num">1</div>
      <div><span class="rite-stage-meta">STEP 1 OF 6</span><h3 class="rite-stage-title">Retrospection</h3></div>
    </div>
    <div class="rite-stage-mlrow"><span class="rite-ml">ആത്മപരിശോധന</span> <span class="rite-tr">Aathma-pari-shodhana</span></div>
    <p class="rite-stage-text">Look inward. Examine your conscience: your actions, words, and thoughts. Where did you fall short? Reflect on the Penitential Psalms (6, 32, 38, 51, 102, 130, 143) to prepare your heart.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="rite-stage" hidden>
    <div class="rite-stage-head">
      <div class="rite-stage-num">2</div>
      <div><span class="rite-stage-meta">STEP 2 OF 6</span><h3 class="rite-stage-title">Repentance</h3></div>
    </div>
    <div class="rite-stage-mlrow"><span class="rite-ml">പശ്ചാത്താപം</span> <span class="rite-tr">Pash-chaat-ta-pam</span></div>
    <p class="rite-stage-text">Feel genuine sorrow, not just fear of punishment, but awareness that your sin has harmed your relationship with God and others.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="rite-stage" hidden>
    <div class="rite-stage-head">
      <div class="rite-stage-num">3</div>
      <div><span class="rite-stage-meta">STEP 3 OF 6</span><h3 class="rite-stage-title">Resolve</h3></div>
    </div>
    <div class="rite-stage-mlrow"><span class="rite-ml">പ്രതിജ്ഞ</span> <span class="rite-tr">Prati-jnja</span></div>
    <p class="rite-stage-text">Vow not to commit those sins again. An honest, firm intention to change your life.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="rite-stage" hidden>
    <div class="rite-stage-head">
      <div class="rite-stage-num">4</div>
      <div><span class="rite-stage-meta">STEP 4 OF 6</span><h3 class="rite-stage-title">Verbal Confession</h3></div>
    </div>
    <div class="rite-stage-mlrow"><span class="rite-ml">കുമ്പസാരം</span> <span class="rite-tr">Kum-pa-sa-ram</span></div>
    <p class="rite-stage-text">Kneel before the priest and recite the <strong>Mawdyonutho</strong> (മൗദ്യാനൂത്തോ), the confession prayer affirming the faith of the three Ecumenical Councils and acknowledging sins in word, deed, and thought. Then confess your sins one by one, clearly and completely. The priest is a witness and guide, not a judge.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="rite-stage" hidden>
    <div class="rite-stage-head">
      <div class="rite-stage-num">5</div>
      <div><span class="rite-stage-meta">STEP 5 OF 6</span><h3 class="rite-stage-title">Absolution</h3></div>
    </div>
    <div class="rite-stage-mlrow"><span class="rite-ml">പാപമോചനം</span> <span class="rite-tr">Paapa-mo-chanam</span></div>
    <p class="rite-stage-text">The priest places his right hand on your head and prays the prayer of absolution, drawing the sign of the cross on your forehead three times, for the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. You respond "Amen" each time.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="rite-stage" hidden>
    <div class="rite-stage-head">
      <div class="rite-stage-num">6</div>
      <div><span class="rite-stage-meta">STEP 6 OF 6</span><h3 class="rite-stage-title">Penance & Communion</h3></div>
    </div>
    <div class="rite-stage-mlrow"><span class="rite-ml">പ്രായശ്ചിത്തം</span> <span class="rite-tr">Praaya-shchit-tam</span></div>
    <p class="rite-stage-text">Complete the penance the priest gives you, then receive the Holy Eucharist at the earliest opportunity. Confession heals; the Eucharist strengthens.</p>
  </section>

<div class="rite-highlight">
  <h3>How Often?</h3>
  <p>The Church encourages confession at least once every 40 days, or at minimum once a year. Great Lent is a natural time. Christians who do not confess at least yearly come under Church discipline.</p>
</div>

<!-- SEAL -->
<div class="confession-section" id="seal">
  <h2 class="confession-section-title">The Seal of Confession</h2>
  <div class="confession-seal-panel">
    <p>Everything you say in confession is kept in <strong>absolute secrecy</strong>. The priest must never reveal what anyone has confessed, not to anyone, for any reason, ever. This protection exists so the faithful can confess freely, without fear.</p>
