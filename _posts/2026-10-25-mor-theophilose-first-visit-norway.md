![First visit of H.E. Dr. Mor Theophilose Kuriakose Metropolitan to Norway]({{ '/assets/img/posts/theophilose-visit-banner-v2.jpg' | relative_url }})

From **23rd to 25th October**, St. Mary's Malankara Syriac Orthodox Church, Oslo, had the joy of welcoming **H.E. Mor Dr. Kuriakose Theophilose** on his first visit to Norway. It was a special and historic weekend for our parish.

## A Warm Welcome

Thirumeni arrived at Oslo Gardermoen Airport on Friday evening. Members of the parish, young and old, were there to greet him with flowers and welcome him to Norway.

## Holy Qurbana and Fellowship

On Saturday morning, the parish gathered at church to receive Thirumeni, with traditional red and gold umbrellas at the door.

![Thirumeni is received at the church door]({{ '/assets/img/posts/theophilose-visit-reception.jpg' | relative_url }})
*Thirumeni is welcomed at the church door.*

The day began with morning prayer (Namaskaram), followed by the Holy Qurbana led by Thirumeni.

![Holy Qurbana led by Thirumeni]({{ '/assets/img/posts/theophilose-visit-holy-qurbana.jpg' | relative_url }})
*Holy Qurbana, with Thirumeni at the altar.*

![Thirumeni during the Holy Qurbana]({{ '/assets/img/posts/theophilose-visit-thirumeni.jpg' | relative_url }})
*Thirumeni during the service.*

After the Qurbana, Thirumeni spoke to the members. This was followed by words of welcome and songs, and then lunch together at church.

## Exploring Oslo

In the afternoon, members joined Thirumeni for a city tour, starting at Vigeland Park. The day ended with dinner together in Oslo.

## Farewell

On Sunday, Thirumeni left from Gardermoen Airport for his return journey.

## Blessings from Thirumeni

Before leaving, Thirumeni wrote a message of thanks and blessing to our parish.

![Thirumeni's handwritten message to the parish, 25 October 2026]({{ '/assets/img/posts/theophilose-visit-letter.jpg' | relative_url }})
*Thirumeni's handwritten message, 25 October 2026.*

> *Glory to be God Almighty,*
>
> It was such a great joy to be with beloved Rev. Fr. Eldhose Vattapparambil and the office bearers and the faithful of our St. Mary's Malankara Syrian Orthodox Congregation in Norway, and to celebrate the Holy Qurbana with them. I was deeply moved by the heartfelt reception, hospitality and love extended by our parishioners.
>
> Let me thank God Almighty for the unity, bond and co-operation of the people in the parish and their active involvement in the church activities and spiritual life. The extraordinary leadership of beloved Eldhose Achen and the dedicated service of the Parish Committee are really commendable.
>
> May God continue to bless our parish and all our parishioners, and thank you all for embracing me in such warm and sincere love and care. May our parish be a blessing for this country and be a good example for the people around as true witness of Christian love and communion.
>
> *With love & blessings*<br>
> **Dr. Kuriakose Theophilose**<br>
> Metropolitan

## Thank You

We thank Thirumeni for his visit, prayers and blessings. A big thank you also to everyone who helped organise the weekend and to all the members and families who took part.
