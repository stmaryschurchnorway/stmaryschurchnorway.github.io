## Walking on Water — Faith Over Fear

Our parish family gathered at Bø i Telemark from 22 to 24 May for the Annual Summer Trip 2026. The weekend was filled with worship, fellowship, and unforgettable memories under the theme *"Walking on Water — Faith Over Fear"* (Matthew 14:27).

## Highlights

- **Holy Qurbana** celebrated at the beautiful Jønnbu Fjellkyrkje on Saturday morning
- **Team competitions** — treasure hunt, quiz, outdoor games, photo story challenge, and theme presentations kept the energy high across all ages
- **Fellowship meals** — from Friday night kanji to Saturday evening grilling and Sunday breakfast waffles, every meal was prepared and shared together
- **Evening worship and prayer** woven through each day, grounding the trip in faith

## Thank You

A heartfelt thank you to everyone who contributed — cooking, cleaning, organising activities, and simply showing up with open hearts. It truly was a weekend of stepping out in faith and growing closer as a community.
