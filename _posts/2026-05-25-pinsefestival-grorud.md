## Pinsefestival på Grorud — Together as God's Family

On 25 May 2026, members of St. Mary's Malankara Syriac Orthodox Church joined the annual **Pinsefestival** (Pentecost Festival) at **Grorud kirke**, Oslo. The festival brought together congregations from across languages, cultures, and Christian traditions under the theme *"Sammen som Guds familie"* — Together as God's Family.

## Our Presence

We had a stand at the festival with a TV displaying the church website, QR codes for visitors to explore the site, and parish members on hand answering questions from those interested in learning more about our community and faith.

## About the Pinsefestival

The Pinsefestival originated in Oslo in 2014 during Norway's Constitution bicentennial and has since expanded to cities across the country. It is organised by **Norges Kristne Råd** (Council of Christian Churches in Norway) in collaboration with Grorud menighet, Groruddalen prostiet, and several other congregations and organisations in Oslo.

The festival addresses themes of inclusion, community, and fellowship — welcoming everyone regardless of background.

## Practical Info

- **Location:** Grorud kirke, Martin Strandlisvei 9, 0956 Oslo
- **Travel:** T-bane or bus to Grorud; parking available at Grorud idrettspark
