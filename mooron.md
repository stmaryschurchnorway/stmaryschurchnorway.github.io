# Holy Mooron (Chrismation)

The Holy Mooron is the Sacrament of the Holy Spirit through which the baptized faithful are sealed with the gift of the Spirit. It is the perfection and fulfillment of Baptism, completing the transformation begun in the waters and empowering the baptized to live the new life in Christ.

The Mooron is a sacred mixture of olive oil, balsam, wine, and forty aromatic substances, consecrated by apostolic authority. It is applied to the entire person in a ritual pattern that sanctifies every aspect of the human being: body, senses, heart, hands, and feet.

The mystery of Chrismation is rooted in the events of Christ's own baptism, when the Holy Spirit descended upon Him, and in the experience of the apostles at Pentecost, when the Spirit was poured out upon the entire Church. What occurred then is personalized for each baptized believer now. The seal of the Mooron marks the faithful forever as belonging to God, sealed as children of the King, soldiers of Christ, and heirs of the Kingdom.
