Understanding Liturgy & Sacraments

† What is Liturgy?

Liturgy means "the work of the people." It is our prayer and worship life shared together—how we gather as a community to praise God, remember Christ's works, and meet His presence. The Holy Qurbana (Divine Liturgy) is our central liturgy, the heart of all worship where we receive the Body and Blood of Christ.

◆ What Are Sacraments?

Sacraments are special celebrations where God meets us. In Syriac, they are rozo—a hidden mystery God reveals. They're real moments when God's love and power work in our lives and change our hearts. We have seven mysteries: baptism, chrismation, eucharist, confession, anointing, marriage, and priesthood. Each marks an important moment on life's journey.

✚ Apostolic & Historical Continuity

The Malankara Orthodox Church's sacramental practice traces directly to the Antiochene liturgical tradition, one of the oldest continuous Christian liturgies. In 1665, under the guidance of Mar Gregorios of Jerusalem, the Church formally adopted Syrian Orthodox liturgical practices, establishing these sacred traditions observed today.

Every sacrament is celebrated through liturgy—the structured prayers, sacred actions, and community worship that form the foundation of our faith. You cannot separate them. Sacraments ARE liturgical celebrations. They are the moments when our common worship becomes visible and tangible in our lives.
