The Seven Holy Mysteries

The Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church inherits one of Christianity's oldest liturgical traditions. Through seven holy sacraments, known as rozo (ܪܳܙܐܳ) or "mystery" in Syriac, celebrated in the ancient prayers and sacred actions of our liturgy, God meets His people at every turning point of life, from birth through eternity.
